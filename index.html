Privacy Policy for Rivet

Developer: Wander Labs · Package ID: com.skylinedigitaldev.rivet · Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Rivet is a single-player mechanical puzzle game built by Wander Labs. Your privacy is simple: Rivet does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal or usage data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No names, email addresses, account logins, or identifiers.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting SDKs, or advertising identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or network requests.

2. Local-Only Game Progress

Puzzle completion state and in-app accessibility/display preferences (such as Light/Dark theme, High Contrast, Reduced Motion, Sound, Haptics, and Tap Controls) are stored strictly on your own device via local application storage and can be cleared at any time using Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the app.

3. Device Permissions

Rivet requests only standard Android vibration feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE) for optional tactile haptics, which can be disabled in Settings.

4. Third-Party Services & Ads

Rivet contains no advertisements and integrates no third-party advertising, social, or tracking SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy

Because Rivet does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children or minors.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Wander Labs at skylinedigitaldev@gmail.com.
